Overview

What is Mechanical System Design & Engineering?

Mechanical system design is the engineering that turns a building's needs into a working set of mechanical services: water supply, pumping, piping, fire protection and plant rooms. It begins with understanding what the building must do and ends with drawings, calculations and specifications that a contractor can build from.

The design starts from load and demand: how much water, how much flow, how much pressure. From there come equipment selection, piping routes, plant room layouts, and the control philosophy that ties it together. Each decision is checked for cost, efficiency, space, safety and maintenance.

Mechanical systems share space with structure, electrical, HVAC and architecture, so coordination is a core part of the work.

  • Design basis document listing codes, standards and assumptions.
  • Calculations for water demand, pipe sizing, pump head and tank capacity.
  • Applicable codes: National Building Code of India, relevant IS standards and local authority rules.

  • Energy efficiency considered in equipment and control selection.
