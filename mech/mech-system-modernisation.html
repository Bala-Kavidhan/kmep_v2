Overview

What is System Modernisation & Performance Upgrades?

Modernisation improves an existing mechanical system without rebuilding the whole building. Older pumps, motors, valves and controls often waste energy, break down more, and may no longer meet current code.

The first step is an audit: measure how the system performs today, find the losses and the weak points, and compare repair against replacement. Typical upgrades include variable speed drives on pumps, high-efficiency motors, replacement of worn pipes and valves, better insulation and improved controls.

Because the building is usually occupied, work is phased and planned to keep services running.

Technical Details

Parameters, standards and practice

  • Baseline measurements of flow, pressure, power and operating hours.
  • Vibration, temperature and thermal imaging checks where useful.
  • Comparison of repair, retrofit and replacement on lifecycle cost.
  • Pump upgrades such as impeller trimming, VFDs or replacement for better efficiency.
  • High-efficiency motors where suitable.
  • Replacement of corroded pipework and leaking valves.
  • Code compliance gaps identified and addressed.
  • Phasing plan to keep services available.
