Overview

What is Piping & Valves?

Piping carries water, refrigerant, steam or other fluids between equipment, and valves control, isolate and protect the flow. Together they form the circulatory system of the building's mechanical services.

Good piping design balances several things: pipe size for acceptable velocity and pressure drop, material for the fluid and temperature, joints that suit the pressure, supports that handle weight and movement, and insulation that stops heat gain or loss and condensation.

Valve selection is just as important. The right valve in the right place makes the system easy to commission, isolate and maintain. The wrong one leaks, sticks or causes noise.

Technical Details

Parameters, standards and practice

  • Pipe velocity guide for water services: roughly 1 to 3 m/s depending on size, with lower values in noise-sensitive areas.
  • Standards for pipes and fittings such as IS 1239 and IS 3589 for steel, IS 4984 for HDPE, and relevant standards for other materials.
  • Valves: gate and ball for isolation, butterfly for larger sizes and regulation, globe for throttling, swing or non-slam check valves to prevent backflow, strainers before pumps and control valves, balancing valves for flow setting.
  • Air vents at high points and drains at low points.
  • Support spacing as per pipe size and material, with anchors and guides to control movement.
  • Insulation with vapour barrier on chilled lines, and cladding in plant rooms and outdoors.
  • Pipes colour-coded as per IS 2379 for easy identification.
  • Hydrostatic test at a pressure above working pressure, typically 1.5 times, held for a set duration with no drop, followed by flushing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do you choose a pipe material?

By fluid, temperature, pressure, location and cost.

Why do chilled water pipes need a vapour barrier?

Without it, moisture enters the insulation and condenses on the cold pipe, causing corrosion and drips.

What is a balancing valve?

A valve used to set the flow in each branch so every unit gets its design flow.

What is flushing?

Circulating water through new pipework to remove debris before connecting equipment.
