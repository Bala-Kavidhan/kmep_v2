Overview

What is Equipment Selection & System Integration?

Equipment selection is choosing the pumps, tanks, valves, heat exchangers and other items that will actually be installed. The right choice depends on duty, efficiency, reliability, space, noise, maintenance and cost over the whole life of the equipment, not only the purchase price.

Each item is selected from performance data: pump curves, efficiency, power, materials of construction and standards. Several makes are compared technically on the same basis so the choice is fair.

System integration then makes sure the selected equipment works with everything around it: power supply, control signals, piping connections, building management system and other trades.

Technical Details

Parameters, standards and practice

  • Data sheets for each item showing duty, efficiency, materials and standards.
  • Motors of high-efficiency class where suitable.
  • Materials chosen for the fluid, such as cast iron, bronze or stainless steel.
  • Noise and vibration limits specified.
  • Foundation loads and dimensions shared with the structural team.
  • Control interface defined: starters, VFDs, sensors and BMS points.
  • Spare parts list and availability checked.
  • Warranty and service support evaluated.
