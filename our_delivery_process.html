How we work

A disciplined path from vision to performance

Every Keerthan MEP project follows a structured engineering framework built around technical rigour, coordinated delivery, transparent communication, and accountable execution.

From initial evaluation to lifecycle support, every decision is made to protect performance, quality, timelines, and long-term value.

01

Consultation & requirement definition

We begin by developing a clear understanding of the client's vision, functional requirements, operational priorities, budget considerations, and performance expectations.

  • Client and stakeholder consultations
  • Project scope and responsibility definition
  • Operational and occupancy requirement analysis
  • Performance and energy-efficiency objectives
  • Preliminary timeline and budget alignment
  • Identification of project-specific constraints
Outcome: A clearly defined project brief aligned with the client's objectives.
02

Site survey & technical assessment

Our engineering team conducts a detailed study of the site and its existing conditions. This establishes an accurate technical foundation before design work begins.

  • Detailed site inspection and measurement
  • Review of architectural and structural information
  • Existing utility and infrastructure assessment
  • Electrical load and HVAC load evaluation
  • Water demand and drainage requirement analysis
  • Space, access, routing, and maintenance assessment
  • Identification of technical risks and site constraints
Outcome: A comprehensive assessment that supports informed design decisions.
03

Concept design & value engineering

Based on the approved requirements, we develop an integrated engineering concept that balances performance, constructability, cost, and lifecycle efficiency.

  • Preliminary system selection and configuration
  • Initial load calculations and capacity planning
  • Equipment and technology evaluation
  • Energy-efficiency and sustainability assessment
  • Capital and operating cost considerations
  • Alternative solution comparison
  • Value-engineering recommendations
Outcome: An optimised engineering concept that delivers the right balance of quality, efficiency, and investment value.
04

Detailed engineering & coordination

The approved concept is developed into coordinated, buildable engineering documentation. Each MEP discipline is carefully integrated with the architectural and structural design.

  • Detailed engineering calculations
  • Equipment selection and technical specifications
  • Coordinated layouts and working drawings
  • Builder's work and service-routing coordination
  • Equipment schedules and material specifications
  • Constructability, access, and maintainability reviews
  • Regulatory and applicable standards review
  • Technical review and design approval
Outcome: A coordinated engineering package ready for procurement and execution.
05

Planning, procurement & project mobilisation

Before work begins on site, we establish a controlled delivery plan covering resources, materials, responsibilities, quality requirements, and project milestones.

  • Detailed project schedule and milestone planning
  • Procurement and material-delivery schedules
  • Vendor and equipment technical evaluation
  • Material submittals and approval coordination
  • Manpower and resource planning
  • Site logistics and work-sequence planning
  • Quality assurance and inspection planning
  • Project communication and reporting framework
Outcome: A fully prepared execution plan that reduces delays, uncertainty, and coordination risks.
06

Installation, quality control & commissioning

Our project team executes the approved design through disciplined site coordination, skilled workmanship, and systematic quality verification.

  • Installation in accordance with approved drawings
  • Coordination with civil, architectural, and other services
  • Routine supervision and progress monitoring
  • Material and workmanship inspections
  • Stage-wise quality checks and documentation
  • Testing, balancing, calibration, and system integration
  • Performance validation against approved criteria
  • Rectification and final readiness verification
Outcome: Fully installed and commissioned systems that perform as intended.
07

Handover & lifecycle support

Project completion is treated as the beginning of the system's operational life. We ensure the client receives the information, guidance, and support required to operate the facility with confidence.

  • Final inspection and snag closure
  • As-built drawings and commissioning records
  • Equipment manuals and warranty documentation
  • Operator orientation and system training
  • Structured project handover
  • Preventive maintenance planning
  • Technical support and performance reviews
  • Energy assessment and system optimisation
Outcome: A confident transition into operations with continued support for efficiency, reliability, and long-term asset performance.

One process. Complete accountability.

At every stage, Keerthan MEP brings together engineering expertise, transparent coordination, and disciplined execution—giving clients confidence from the first conversation through years of operation.
