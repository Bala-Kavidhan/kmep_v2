Overview

What is Water-Efficient Plumbing Solutions?

Water-efficient plumbing delivers the same comfort and hygiene while using less water. It reduces water bills, pumping energy and the load on sewers and treatment plants, and it protects scarce water resources.

Efficiency comes from several layers. The simplest is the fixture: dual-flush toilets, aerated taps and efficient showers cut use at the point of use. Pressure control prevents fixtures from running at high flow. Metering and leak detection reveal where water is going. Reuse of treated or grey water for flushing and landscaping replaces fresh water where drinking quality is not needed.

Water efficiency also supports green building ratings such as IGBC and GRIHA, which award credit for lower consumption.

Technical Details

Parameters, standards and practice

  • Fixture flow rates set as targets, for example dual-flush WCs of around 3 and 6 litres and taps and showers with flow limits.
  • Fixtures with a recognised water-efficiency label where available.
  • Pressure-reducing valves where supply pressure is high.
  • Water meters on main inlets, borewells, STP outlets and major blocks.
  • Leak detection through night-flow checks and meter comparison.
  • Dual plumbing for flushing with treated water, clearly marked.
  • Landscape irrigation by drip or sprinklers using treated water.
  • Rainwater harvesting integrated with the system.
  • Calculations for IGBC and GRIHA water credits where required.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will low-flow fixtures reduce comfort?

Well-designed fixtures keep comfort. Aerators and good spray design maintain the feel.

Is greywater safe to reuse?

For flushing and landscaping, after suitable treatment. It is not for drinking.

What is sub-metering?

Separate meters on parts of the building, to see and charge for actual use.

How much can be saved?

It depends on the building and the starting point. A baseline calculation shows the likely saving.
