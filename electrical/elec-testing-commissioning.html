Overview

What is Testing, Commissioning & Compliance Verification?

Testing and commissioning proves that an electrical installation is safe to energise, performs as designed and meets the standards and legal requirements that apply. It is the final quality gate before power is switched on and the building is handed over.

Tests are done in stages. Before energising, electricians check insulation resistance, continuity and polarity, and confirm earthing and protection settings. After energising, they check voltages, phase sequence and load, test protection devices and run functional tests of generators, UPS, lighting controls and BMS integration.

Compliance verification then collects the records, certificates and drawings needed for approval by the Electrical Inspectorate and, where required, the fire authority.

Technical Details

Parameters, standards and practice

  • Tests carried out to IS 732, IS 3043 and manufacturer's instructions.
  • Calibrated test instruments with valid certificates.
  • Insulation resistance measured with a megger at the voltage suitable for the circuit.
  • Earth resistance measured for each earth pit and for the combined system.
  • Protection settings verified against the coordination study.
  • Transformer tests including ratio, insulation and oil tests where applicable.
  • Generator load test and UPS autonomy test.
  • Lux survey of the lighting installation.
  • Thermographic scan under load.
  • Statutory approval from the Electrical Inspectorate, and fire clearance where required.
  • Test records signed and retained for handover.
