Overview

What is LV Power Distribution?

Power distribution is the physical network that carries electricity from the main panel through risers and floors to each distribution board and load. It includes cables, cable trays, busbar trunking, conduits, glands, earthing and metering.

The choice between cable and busbar trunking depends on current, route and flexibility. Cables suit most feeders. Busbar trunking suits high-current risers and long runs with many tap-offs, and is easy to reconfigure. Both must be sized for current, voltage drop, short-circuit withstand and the effects of grouping and temperature.

Installation quality matters as much as design. Correct bending radii, secure supports, proper terminations, segregation of services and fire stopping at penetrations all affect safety and reliability.

Technical Details

Parameters, standards and practice

  • Cables to relevant IS standards such as IS 7098 for XLPE and IS 694 for PVC.
  • Conductor material of copper or aluminium chosen by load, route and cost.
  • Derating factors applied for grouping, ambient temperature and installation method.
  • Voltage drop and short-circuit withstand checked for every feeder.
  • Cable bending radius and pulling tension respected.
  • Segregation of power, control and data cables.
  • Fire stopping at wall and slab penetrations.
  • Cable colour coding and tagging for easy identification.
  • Earthing to IS 3043 with proper bonding of all metal parts.
  • Metering with energy meters and multifunction meters for monitoring.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When should busbar trunking be used instead of cable?

For high currents and long vertical or horizontal runs with several tap-offs.

What is FRLS cable?

Flame retardant, low smoke cable that limits fire spread and smoke.

Why derate cables?

Cables grouped together or in hot places cannot carry as much current.

What is the purpose of cable tagging?

To identify every circuit quickly for maintenance and testing.
