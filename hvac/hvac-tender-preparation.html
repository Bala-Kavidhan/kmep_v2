Overview

What is Tender Value Engineering?

Value engineering is a structured review of a design to achieve the required function at lower cost, or better function at the same cost. It is not simply cutting. Every change is checked against performance, safety and maintenance impact.

At tender stage, the design, specification and Bill of Quantities are reviewed together. Over-specified equipment, duplicated items, unnecessary redundancy and costly details are identified, and equivalent alternatives are proposed.

The result is a leaner tender package with clear justification for each change, and bids that can be compared fairly.

Parameters, standards and practice

  • Lifecycle view: lower first cost must not cause higher running cost.
