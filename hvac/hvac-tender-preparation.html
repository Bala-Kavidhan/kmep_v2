Overview

What is Tender Value Engineering?

Value engineering is a structured review of a design to achieve the required function at lower cost, or better function for the same cost. It is not simply cutting. Every change is checked against performance, safety and maintenance.

At tender stage, we review the design, specification and Bill of Quantities together. Over-specified equipment, duplicated items and costly details are identified, and equivalent alternatives are proposed.

The result is a leaner tender package with a clear reason for each change.

Technical Details

Our Review Approach

  • Function first: each item is checked against what it must achieve.
  • Lifecycle view: a lower first cost must not lead to a higher running cost.
  • Equivalent alternatives supported by manufacturer data sheets.
  • Quantities checked against drawings to remove errors and double counting.
  • Risk review of each proposed change.
  • Savings shown item by item, with the reason.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does value engineering reduce quality?

The aim is equal performance at lower cost. Each change is justified.

When should it be done?

Ideally before the tender is released, but it can also be done on submitted bids.

Who approves the changes?

The client and the design consultant.

Can savings be guaranteed?

Savings depend on the design. We report what is found.
