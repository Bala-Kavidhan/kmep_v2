Overview

What is Heat Load Calculation?

Heat load calculation is the process of finding out how much cooling or heating each space in a building needs to stay comfortable. It is the first engineering step in any HVAC project.

The result depends on the building's orientation, walls, roof and glass, the number of people, lighting and equipment, and the fresh air required. Every later decision, such as equipment capacity, duct sizes and electrical load, is based on it.

A proper calculation replaces guesswork with room-wise figures that can be reviewed and relied on.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is heat load calculation?

It is the process of finding out how much cooling each space needs, so the right HVAC equipment can be selected.

Why not estimate the capacity by area?

Heat load changes with glass area, orientation, number of people and how the space is used. A single estimate for all rooms can lead to oversized or undersized equipment.

What information do you need to start?

Building plans, construction details, occupancy, lighting and equipment information, and the operating hours of the building.

When should the calculation be repeated?

When the layout, occupancy or use of the space changes, or when new equipment is added.
