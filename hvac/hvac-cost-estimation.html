Overview

What is an HVAC Proposal?

An HVAC proposal turns a design into a clear scope, price and schedule. A good proposal lets you understand exactly what is included and compare options fairly.

We prepare each proposal from the site survey, the cooling requirement and your needs, not from a standard template. Equipment is selected to suit the building, and quantities are taken from the layouts.

Where more than one approach is possible, we present the options side by side so the decision is easy.

Our Process

Our Proposal Process

1

Understand the Requirement

Discuss how the space will be used, your expectations, budget and timeline.

2

Survey the Site

Record measurements, existing services, access and power availability.

3

Select the System

Choose suitable equipment based on the cooling requirement.

4

Prepare the BOQ

List quantities and rates for equipment, piping, ducting, insulation, electrical work and controls.

5

Develop Options

Prepare alternatives with their cost and performance differences.

6

Finalise the Proposal

Define the scope, schedule and terms, then review them with you.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What do you need to prepare a proposal?

Architectural plans if available, details of how the space will be used, and access for a site survey.

Is the price fixed?

Prices are valid for the period stated in the proposal. Changes in scope are priced separately.

Can you offer alternatives?

Yes, usually a main option along with one or two alternatives.

Are taxes included?

Each proposal states clearly whether taxes are included.
