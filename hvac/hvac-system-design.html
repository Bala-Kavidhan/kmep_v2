Overview

What is HVAC System Design?

HVAC system design is the process of choosing and planning the complete air-conditioning arrangement for a building. It decides how cooling is produced, how it reaches each space and how it is controlled.

No single system suits every building. Split units suit small spaces, VRF systems suit buildings with many zones, packaged units suit large open areas, and chilled water systems suit large buildings and campuses. The right choice depends on the size and use of the building, available space, budget and maintenance needs.

We compare the suitable options, then plan the chosen system in full, including equipment, piping, controls and fresh air.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose between VRF and chilled water?

It depends mainly on the size of the building, how it is used, the space available and the lifetime cost. VRF suits buildings with many zones, while chilled water suits large buildings.

Why is zoning important?

Rooms with different usage need separate control, so each area stays comfortable without wasting energy.

Can an existing system be redesigned?

Yes. We review the existing system and suggest improvements or replacement.

Do you design the controls?

Yes. This includes zoning, scheduling and connection to the building management system.
