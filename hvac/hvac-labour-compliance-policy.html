Overview

What is Our Labour Compliance Policy?

Contracting sites depend on many workers, and the law gives those workers rights to fair wages, social security, safe conditions and lawful employment. Compliance protects the workers, the client and the contractor.

Our policy is to follow the labour laws that apply on every site. This includes registration and contributions under the Employees’ State Insurance (ESI) scheme and the Employees’ Provident Fund (EPF), payment of at least the applicable minimum wages, proper records and the required contractor licences.

Requirements change, so we review them regularly and keep documents ready for client audits.

Technical Details

What We Maintain

  • Compliance with applicable central and state labour laws.
  • ESI and EPF registration, with monthly payment records.
  • Wage payment through bank where applicable.
  • Contractor licence and registration documents available for inspection.
  • Insurance cover for workers as required.
  • No child or forced labour.
  • Subcontractor labour checked to the same standard.
  • Documents kept for the period required by law.
