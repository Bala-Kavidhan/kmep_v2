Overview

What is Labour Compliance Policy?

Contracting sites depend on large numbers of workers, and the law gives those workers rights: fair wages, social security, safe conditions and lawful employment. Compliance protects workers, the client and the contractor.

Our policy is to follow applicable labour laws and rules on every site. This includes registration and contributions under the Employees' State Insurance (ESI) scheme and the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF), payment of at least the applicable minimum wages, maintenance of attendance and wage records, and holding required contractor licences and registrations.

Requirements change, so we review them regularly and keep documentation ready for client audits.

Technical Details

Parameters, standards and practice

  • Compliance with applicable central and state labour laws and codes.
  • ESI and EPF registration and monthly remittance records.
  • Minimum wage compliance and payment through bank where applicable.
  • Contractor licence and registration documents available for inspection.
  • Insurance cover for workers as required.
  • No child or forced labour.
  • Subcontractor labour checked to the same standard.
  • Documents retained for the period required by law.
