Overview

What is Measurement Analysis?

Measurement analysis, often called testing and balancing, checks that an installed HVAC system performs as designed. Equipment can be installed correctly and still deliver the wrong airflow to rooms.

We measure airflow, adjust dampers so each room receives its intended supply, and record temperature and humidity. The results are compared with the design.

The same process is used to investigate existing systems that have comfort complaints or high energy use.

Technical Details

How We Measure

  • Readings taken at ducts and air terminals with calibrated instruments.
  • Capture hoods, anemometers, manometers, thermo-hygrometers and clamp meters used as required.
  • System made complete, with clean filters and open fire dampers, before balancing.
  • Total airflow checked at the fan and compared with the sum at the terminals.
  • Temperature readings taken at working height across the space.
  • Motor current checked to confirm fan loading.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When should balancing be done?

After installation and before handover, or whenever comfort problems arise.

Can you audit an existing system?

Yes. We measure the system and report where it differs from its intended performance.

What does CFM mean?

CFM stands for cubic feet per minute, a measure of airflow.

Does balancing reduce energy use?

Often yes, because fans no longer need to over-deliver.
