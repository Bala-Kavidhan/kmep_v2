Overview

What is Our Environmental Policy?

HVAC affects the environment in two main ways: the energy used for cooling, and the refrigerants that can leak from equipment. Both need careful management.

Our policy is to design and install efficient systems, choose lower-impact refrigerants where suitable, prevent leaks, recover refrigerant during service and manage site waste responsibly.

It applies to our design work, site activities and after sales service.

Technical Details

Our Commitments

  • Only trained persons handle refrigerant.
  • Recovery equipment is used during service work.
  • Pressure and vacuum testing reduces the risk of leaks.
  • Packaging, metal, insulation and oils are separated for reuse or authorised disposal.
  • Water treatment and efficient cooling tower operation where applicable.
  • Rules on refrigerant handling that apply to the project are followed.
