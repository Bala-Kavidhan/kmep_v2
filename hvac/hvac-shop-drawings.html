Overview

What is AutoCAD Drafting?

Drawings are how engineering intent reaches the site. Clear, coordinated drawings let installers work correctly the first time, and help clients and consultants approve work quickly.

We prepare HVAC drawings in AutoCAD at several levels: design drawings that show the concept, shop drawings that show exactly how each duct, pipe and unit will be installed, and as-built drawings that record what was actually built.

Good drafting is consistent. Layers, line types, symbols, title blocks and revision tracking follow one standard across the project.

Technical Details

Parameters, standards and practice

  • Typical scales: 1:100 for plans, 1:50 for plant rooms and congested areas, with larger scale for details.
  • Layer naming and colours standardised for ducts, pipes, equipment and annotations.
  • Elevations and levels shown for ducts and pipes.
  • Penetration and sleeve locations marked for civil work.
  • Title block with project, drawing number, revision and date.
  • Files supplied in DWG and PDF.
