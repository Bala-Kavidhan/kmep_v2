Overview

What is Health & Safety Policy?

HVAC installation involves heavy equipment, work at height, electrical connections, hot work such as brazing and welding, confined shafts and pressurised refrigerant. Each carries real risk.

Our Health & Safety policy commits to preventing injury and illness. It sets rules for planning work, training crews, controlling hazards and learning from incidents. Safety comes before schedule.

The policy applies to all employees, subcontractors and visitors on our sites.

Our Process

How we do it, step by step

1

Plan and assess

Hazard identification and risk assessment before work starts.

2

Site induction and task-specific training for every worker.

3

Issue permits

Permit to work for hot work, work at height, confined space and electrical isolation.

4

Control hazards

PPE, guarding, barricading, lock-out/tag-out and safe lifting.

5

Supervise and inspect

Daily supervision and periodic safety inspections.

6

Report and learn

Report incidents and near-misses, investigate, and share lessons.

Technical Details

Parameters, standards and practice

  • Personal protective equipment: helmet, safety shoes, gloves, eye protection, and harness for work at height.
  • Permit to work system for high-risk activities.
  • Lock-out/tag-out before working on electrical or rotating equipment.
  • Safe handling and storage of refrigerant cylinders and gases.
  • Lifting plans and certified lifting equipment for heavy items.
  • Housekeeping, fire extinguishers and first-aid provision on site.
  • Emergency response plan and contact list.
  • Incident reporting, investigation and corrective action.
