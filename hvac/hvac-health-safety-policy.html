Overview

What is Our Health & Safety Policy?

HVAC installation involves heavy equipment, work at height, electrical connections, hot work and pressurised refrigerant. Each carries real risk if it is not managed.

Our policy commits us to preventing injury and illness. It sets out how work is planned, how crews are trained, how hazards are controlled and how incidents are learned from. Safety comes before schedule.

The policy applies to all employees, subcontractors and visitors on our sites.

Why It Matters

Why is Site Safety Important?

Work at Height

Ladders, scaffolds, roofs and shafts need controlled access.

Electrical Hazards

Live panels and temporary supplies must be isolated and controlled.

Fire and Burns

Brazing and welding need permits and fire precautions.

Heavy Lifting

Chillers, air handling units and outdoor units need planned lifting.

Our Process

Our Safety Process

1

Plan and Assess

Identify hazards and assess risks before work starts.

2

Induct and Train

Give every worker a site induction and task-specific training.

3

Issue Permits

Use permits for hot work, work at height, confined spaces and electrical isolation.

4

Control Hazards

Apply protective equipment, barricading, lock-out and safe lifting.

5

Supervise and Inspect

Supervise daily and carry out regular safety inspections.

6

Report and Learn

Report incidents and near-misses, investigate them and share the lessons.

Technical Details

Site Safety Requirements

  • Helmet, safety shoes, gloves, eye protection and a harness where needed.
  • Lock-out and tag-out before work on electrical or rotating equipment.
  • Safe storage and handling of refrigerant cylinders and gases.
  • Certified lifting equipment and lifting plans for heavy items.
  • Good housekeeping, with fire extinguishers available on site.
  • An emergency response plan with a contact list.
  • Investigation of incidents, with corrective action.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is safety training given to all workers?

Yes, through site induction and regular toolbox talks.

What is a permit to work?

A written authorisation for high-risk tasks that states the hazards and the controls.

How are incidents handled?

They are reported, investigated and used to improve our procedures.

Does the policy apply to subcontractors?

Yes. Subcontractors follow the same safety rules as our own crews.
