Overview

What is a Method Statement?

A method statement is a written procedure that explains how a task will be carried out, step by step, along with the safety and quality controls that apply. It is usually submitted to the client or consultant for approval before work begins.

For HVAC work, method statements are prepared for activities such as equipment installation, ductwork, piping, insulation, testing and commissioning.

Each one is specific to the task and the site, and is explained to the crew doing the work.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which activities need a method statement?

Typically installation, testing and any high-risk work.

Can you follow our format?

Yes, we can adapt to client templates.

How is it different from a risk assessment?

The method statement describes how to do the work. The risk assessment lists the hazards and their controls. They are used together.

Who approves it?

The client or consultant, as set out in the contract.
