Overview

What is Method Statement?

A method statement is a written procedure that explains how a task will be carried out, step by step, with the safety and quality controls that apply. It is usually submitted to the client or consultant for approval before work begins.

For HVAC, typical method statements cover chiller and AHU installation, duct installation, pipe installation and pressure testing, refrigerant piping and brazing, insulation, and testing and commissioning.

A good method statement is specific to the task and the site, and is understood by the crew doing the work.

Which activities need a method statement?

Typically installation, testing and any high-risk work.

Can you follow our format?

Yes, we can adapt to client templates.

How is it different from a risk assessment?

The method statement describes how to do the work. The risk assessment lists hazards and controls. They are used together.

Who approves it?

The client or consultant, as per the contract.
