Overview

What is Contract Execution?

Contract execution is the stage where drawings become a working installation. It covers procurement, site mobilisation, installation, coordination with other trades, testing and handover.

HVAC work depends on others. Civil work must leave openings, electrical must provide power, plumbing must provide drain points, and interiors must leave ceiling access. Execution therefore needs a clear programme, regular coordination and quality checks at every stage.

We plan the work in stages, track progress against the schedule, and complete testing and commissioning before handover.

Our Process

How we do it, step by step

1

Contract review and planning

Confirm scope, drawings, schedule and responsibilities. Prepare the programme and procurement plan.

2

Submittals and approvals

Material, equipment and drawing approvals before ordering.

3

Procurement

Order equipment and materials in line with the programme.

4

Mobilisation

Site setup, safety induction, storage and manpower.

5

Installation

Sleeves and supports, equipment placement, piping and ducting, insulation and electrical connections.

6

Inspection and testing

Pressure tests, leak tests, flushing, air balancing and functional tests.

7

Commissioning and handover

Performance verification, training, as-built drawings and manuals.
