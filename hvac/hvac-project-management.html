Overview

What is Contract Execution?

Contract execution is the stage where approved drawings become a working installation. It covers procurement, site mobilisation, installation, coordination with other trades, testing and handover.

HVAC work depends on other trades. Civil work must leave openings, electrical must provide power, plumbing must provide drain points and interiors must leave ceiling access. Execution therefore needs a clear programme and regular coordination.

We plan the work in stages, track progress against the schedule and complete testing before handover.
