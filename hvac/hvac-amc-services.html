Overview

What is After Sales Service?

After-sales service keeps HVAC equipment performing as designed after handover. Air-conditioning plant runs for long hours, collects dust, and has moving parts, so it needs regular attention.

An Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) sets out scheduled servicing, response times for breakdowns and what is covered. Preventive visits find small faults before they cause failures, keep coils and filters clean for efficiency, and extend equipment life.

We tailor each AMC to the equipment, the usage and how critical cooling is to your operation.

Technical Details

Parameters, standards and practice

  • Comprehensive AMC covers labour and agreed spares. Non-comprehensive covers labour and consumables only.
  • Response time targets agreed by priority, for example critical areas first.
  • Refrigerant leak checks and top-up records.
  • Log of readings to track trends, such as rising current or falling cooling.
  • Cooling tower and chilled water treatment where applicable.
  • Spare parts list with lead times for critical items.

Typical maintenance schedule

FrequencyTypical tasks
Monthly / every visitClean or replace air filters, check drain lines, check thermostats and controls, listen for abnormal noise and vibration.
QuarterlyClean indoor and outdoor coils, check refrigerant pressures, check electrical connections and motor current, lubricate bearings, check belts.
Half-yearlyCheck fan balance and alignment, inspect insulation, test safety cut-outs, clean strainers.
YearlyChiller condenser and evaporator tube cleaning, full performance test, cooling tower cleaning and water treatment review, panel inspection and tightening.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between comprehensive and non-comprehensive AMC?

Comprehensive includes spare parts replacement as agreed. Non-comprehensive covers service labour and consumables, with parts charged separately.

How often should servicing be done?

Usually monthly or quarterly depending on equipment and usage.

Do you attend emergency calls?

Yes, with response times set in the contract.
