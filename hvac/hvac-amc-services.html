Overview

What is After Sales Service?

After sales service keeps HVAC equipment performing as designed once a project is handed over. Air-conditioning equipment runs for long hours and needs regular care to stay efficient and dependable.

Our service is built around an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC), which sets out the scheduled visits, the response to breakdowns and the equipment covered.

Each contract is tailored to your equipment, how it is used and how important cooling is to your operation.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AMC?

An Annual Maintenance Contract is an agreement for regular servicing and breakdown support for your equipment over a year.

What is the difference between comprehensive and labour-only plans?

Comprehensive plans include agreed spare parts. Labour-only plans cover service work, and parts are charged separately.

How often is servicing done?

The frequency depends on the equipment and how it is used, and is set in the contract.

Can you service equipment installed by others?

Yes. Share the equipment details and we will assess it before agreeing a plan.
