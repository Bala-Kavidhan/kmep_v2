Overview

What is Duct Design?

Duct design is the process of planning the ducts that carry conditioned air, fresh air and exhaust air throughout a building. It helps ensure that every room receives the required amount of air.

A good duct layout considers room requirements, duct sizes, air distribution, noise levels and available ceiling space. It also ensures that the ductwork is properly connected to air handling units and other HVAC equipment.

Proper planning helps avoid airflow problems, unnecessary noise and conflicts with other building services during installation.

Technical Details

Design Considerations

  • Duct sizes selected based on the required airflow and available space.
  • Air velocity and pressure requirements considered for comfortable operation.
  • Suitable duct materials and thickness selected according to project requirements.
  • Insulation provided where required to prevent heat gain and condensation.
  • Fire dampers provided where ducts pass through fire-rated walls and floors, as required.
  • Noise and vibration control considered during duct planning.
  • Proper access provided for dampers, filters and maintenance requirements.
  • Duct supports, sealing and installation clearances considered in the design.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the purpose of duct design?

Duct design ensures that conditioned air, fresh air and exhaust air are properly distributed throughout a building.

Why is duct sizing important?

Correct duct sizing helps maintain the required airflow, reduce noise and avoid unnecessary pressure losses.

What is the difference between supply and return air ducts?

Supply ducts deliver conditioned air into rooms, while return ducts carry room air back to the HVAC equipment.

Do all ducts require insulation?

Not all ducts require insulation. It depends on the air temperature, surrounding conditions and project requirements.

Do you provide duct drawings?

Yes. Duct layout drawings, sections, schedules and installation details can be provided as part of the design deliverables.
