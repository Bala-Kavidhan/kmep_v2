Overview

What is Quality Policy?

Quality means the installation does what it was designed to do, lasts as long as intended and can be proved to the client. In HVAC, many defects are hidden behind ceilings, in shafts and under insulation, so checks must happen before work is covered.

Our Quality Policy sets out what is inspected, when, by whom and how it is recorded. It covers incoming material, installation stages, testing and handover.

Records make every result traceable.

Technical Details

Parameters, standards and practice

  • Inspection and test plan prepared before work starts.
  • Material inspection request raised and recorded before use.
  • Hold points where work cannot proceed until inspected.
  • Calibrated instruments for testing.
  • Non-conformance reports with root cause and corrective action.
  • Records retained and handed over.

Inspection and test stages

StageWhat is checked
Incoming materialMake, model, size, test certificates and condition against the approved submittal.
Pipe installationAlignment, slope, supports and joints before insulation.
Pressure testWater piping tested to a pressure and duration set by specification, typically above working pressure, with no drop or leaks.
Refrigerant pipingNitrogen pressure test, then vacuum to the level required by the manufacturer before charging.
DuctworkSealing, supports, dampers and leak testing where specified.
EquipmentLevelling, vibration isolation, electrical connection and run test.
HandoverPerformance readings and snag list closure.
