Overview

What is Our Quality Policy?

Quality means an installation does what it was designed to do, lasts as long as intended and can be proven to the client. Many HVAC defects are hidden behind ceilings, in shafts and under insulation, so checks must be made before work is covered.

Our Quality Policy sets out what is inspected, when, by whom and how it is recorded. It covers incoming material, installation stages, testing and handover.

Every result is recorded so that it can be traced.

Technical Details

Our Quality Controls

  • An inspection and test plan prepared before work starts.
  • Material inspection requests raised and recorded before use.
  • Hold points where work cannot continue until it is inspected.
  • Calibrated instruments used for testing.
  • Non-conformance reports with cause and corrective action.
  • Records kept and handed over to the client.
