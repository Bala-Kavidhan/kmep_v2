Overview

What is Supervision & Document Control?

Supervision ensures that work on site follows approved drawings and specifications. Document control ensures that everyone is using the right version of every drawing and that every approval, test and instruction is recorded.

On HVAC projects, drawings change often because of coordination with other trades. If crews work from an old revision, work must be redone. Records also protect both client and contractor if questions arise later.

Our system tracks documents from issue to approval to as-built, and logs every inspection and test.

Technical Details

Parameters, standards and practice

  • Document numbering and revision scheme applied to every drawing.
  • Transmittals record what was sent, to whom and when.
  • Approval status for each submittal, for example approved, approved with comments, or revise and resubmit.
  • Request for Information (RFI) log with response dates.
  • Inspection requests and test reports linked to locations.
  • Non-conformance reports tracked to closure.
  • Daily site diary kept by the supervisor.
  • Backup and controlled access to digital files.
