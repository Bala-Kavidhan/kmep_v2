Overview

What is Supervision & Document Control?

Supervision ensures that site work follows approved drawings and specifications. Document control ensures that everyone uses the correct version of every drawing and that every approval, inspection and test is recorded.

On HVAC projects, drawings change often because of coordination with other trades. If crews work from an old revision, the work has to be redone. Good records also protect both client and contractor if questions arise later.

Our system tracks documents from issue to approval to as-built, and logs every inspection and test.

How We Control Documents

  • A numbering and revision scheme applied to every drawing.
  • Transmittals that record what was sent, to whom and when.
  • Clear approval status for each submittal.
  • A Request for Information (RFI) log with response dates.
  • Inspection requests and test reports linked to locations.
  • A daily site diary kept by the supervisor.
  • Backup and controlled access to digital files.

What is an RFI?

A formal request for information or clarification, recorded together with its answer.

Are records kept digitally?

Yes, in a controlled structure with backup.
