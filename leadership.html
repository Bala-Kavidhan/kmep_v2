With more than 26 years of experience in the HVAC industry, Rajesh Subburaj brings extensive technical knowledge, commercial perspective, and client-focused leadership to Keerthan MEP Engineers. He holds a Post Diploma in Refrigeration and Air Conditioning, which established the foundation for his career in climate engineering.

Before joining Keerthan MEP, Rajesh held key roles with Voltas and LG Electronics, gaining valuable experience across HVAC technologies, technical solution development, and client engagement. His ability to understand complex requirements and translate them into practical, performance-led solutions has contributed to dependable project outcomes and lasting client relationships.

As Business Head, Rajesh leads with a strong emphasis on engineering integrity, responsive service, and complete accountability. His approach brings technical and commercial priorities into alignment—ensuring every solution delivers meaningful value throughout its lifecycle.
